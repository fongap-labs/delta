# Naming Conventions

Canonical cross-repository naming rules are maintained in:

- https://github.com/fongap/action-worker/blob/main/docs/NAMING_CONVENTIONS.md

This repository does not duplicate the central naming specification.

Project-local static enforcement may remain in `scripts/check_naming.py` where it validates source-language or repository-specific details that are not yet covered by the central validator. Local enforcement may be stricter, but it must not redefine or contradict the central naming semantics.
